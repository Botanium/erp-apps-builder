# 05 — Compile once and provision retail and cafe independently

**What to build:** An owner can request governed provisioning of one exact Approved Blueprint, have it compile once into a target-neutral Effective Blueprint, and see retail and cafe prepared and activated independently with exact Applied Blueprint truth.

**Blocked by:** 04 — Approve one exact Blueprint through a Human Gate.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Approved Blueprint selects the closed v1 Capability inventory with exact versions: retail enables its required ten Capabilities and cafe its required nine, while all eight shared Capabilities use identical identities, versions, governed actions, and invariants.
- [ ] The Business Kernel compiles the exact validated Blueprint and bound version set once into one deterministic, immutable, self-contained, content-identified Effective Blueprint.
- [ ] Compilation resolves only declared dependencies, contracts, bindings, Policy Profiles, experience manifests, adapters, invariants, and traceability; it creates no business Records, Events, stock, ledger, Payment, approval, or target authority.
- [ ] Each clean target receives its own fresh Initial Provision Compatible verdict bound to its exact empty baseline and governing version set.
- [ ] Each Provisioning Request binds the current Approved Blueprint, approval record, Content Identity, compatibility verdict, expected Applied baseline, target, versions, and a unique idempotency identity.
- [ ] Preparation builds and validates the complete candidate experience in isolation without changing active configuration or business truth.
- [ ] Activation uses one target-specific atomic compare-and-set and records the exact Applied Blueprint Reference, Content Identity, successful attempt, activation time, and version set.
- [ ] Retail and cafe attempts progress and fail independently; a combined owner view exposes partial success without claiming both targets are Applied.
- [ ] Capability dispatch is selected by governed-action identity and contains no Retail Capability, Cafe application Capability, Tenant label branch, generated tenant code, or arbitrary extension.
- [ ] Public-behavior tests prove one compilation, two independent activations, exact replay behavior, and no authority from a merely Prepared candidate.
